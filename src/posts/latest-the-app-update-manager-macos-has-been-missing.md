<p>For those apps that <a href="https://sindresorhus.com/supercharge" data-type="link" data-id="https://sindresorhus.com/supercharge">push the limits of macOS</a>, they&#8217;re sometimes not distributed via the App Store due to Apple&#8217;s restrictions. When I have a bunch of such apps alongside the apps I&#8217;ve downloaded from the Mac App Store, there&#8217;s no central way for me to see and update them to the latest versions. This is where <a href="https://max.codes/latest/" data-type="link" data-id="https://max.codes/latest/">Latest</a> helps me manage app updates seamlessly. The app asks you to choose the folders where your apps are installed and then scans them for updates.</p>



<p>Latest is a free and open source app for macOS that acts as a unified dashboard for all my app updates. Rather than me bouncing between the Mac App Store and individual app updaters, I get a single interface that shows everything that needs my attention.</p>


<div class="wp-block-image">
<figure class="aligncenter"><img decoding="async" src="/wp-content/uploads/recovered/craft-3.png" alt="Image.png"/></figure>
</div>


<p>I can update the macOS apps natively form inside the Latest app, which is great. I can see what&#8217;s new (release notes) and check version numbers. The app pulls this information from the Sparkle framework (open source) used in Mac apps, which according to developer <a href="https://max.codes/" data-type="link" data-id="https://max.codes/">Max Langer</a> covers most apps outside the Mac App Store.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Key features</h2>



<p>The app is very simple and clever. I can update a single app or update all at once (keyboard shortcuts are available). One thing that&#8217;s missing from the macOS App Store&#8217;s &#8220;Updates&#8221; section is the option to manually refresh and look for new updates. Latest addresses this gap perfectly.</p>



<p>What I particularly like about Latest:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Unified update management</li>



<li>Release notes integration</li>



<li>Clean, native interface</li>



<li>Open source and free</li>
</ul>


<div class="wp-block-image">
<figure class="aligncenter"><img decoding="async" src="/wp-content/uploads/recovered/craft-1.png" alt="Image.png"/></figure>

<p>With the popular <a href="https://www.corecode.io/macupdater/" data-type="link" data-id="https://www.corecode.io/macupdater/">MacUpdater</a> being discontinued by the end of this year, Latest becomes an even more valuable alternative for me.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Trade-offs</h2>



<p>Some apps need to be opened manually for updates, which isn&#8217;t ideal but is a necessary tradeoff. And some apps require redownloading from their website rather than updating in place. These limitations aren&#8217;t deal-breakers for me, but they&#8217;re worth noting. I can also ignore an app to skip checking for updates.</p>



<p>Latest fills a genuine gap in the macOS ecosystem by bringing much-needed organization to app updates across different sources. For a free, open-source utility created by a single developer, it punches well above its weight.</p>



<p>Since I find myself forgetting to update applications or getting frustrated with the scattered nature of Mac app updates, Latest is absolutely worthy of keeping in my dock. It&#8217;s one of those simple utilities that quietly makes my Mac experience better without getting in my way.</p>
